02 — An honest promise

A sky guide, not a magic telescope

SkyLens is

  • A guide that names what you see
  • A planner for when and where to look
  • A logbook for your observations
  • A doorway to real citizen science

SkyLens is not

  • A replacement for a telescope
  • Live zoomed-in video of planets
  • A promise you will find a new planet
  • An AI that makes facts up

05 — How it works

How does the phone know where you are pointing?

A gravity sensor feels which way is down, so it knows how high you are aiming.

A compass feels the Earth's magnetic north, so it knows which way you face.

Compasses wander near metal. Line up the Moon once and SkyLens corrects itself.
