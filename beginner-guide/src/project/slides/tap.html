Step four: tap anything. A card slides up from the bottom. It says what the object is in plain words, gives its rise time, its highest point tonight and how bright it is. Best of all, it gives directions a beginner can follow: face south-east, look about three fists above the horizon. Hold your fist out at arm's length and each fist is roughly ten degrees, so you can find the object even with the phone in your pocket. Each card also shows where its facts came from.
