A fair question from a beginner: can I trust where it says things are? SkyLens carries 2,865 stars, every one bright enough to see without a telescope, the 88 constellation figures, plus the Sun, the Moon and the seven other planets. The sky part needs no internet at all. And the calculations were tested against the worked examples in Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, the standard reference book. Results agree to within a few arcminutes. For scale, the full Moon is about thirty arcminutes across, so the error is far smaller than anything your eye can notice.
