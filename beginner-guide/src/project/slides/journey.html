Phone screens on the next slides are animated illustrations. Real results depend on where and when you look.

Here is the journey we will follow. Imagine it is 9 pm and you are in your garden or on a rooftop. Step one, you open the app. Step two, it shows what is up tonight. Step three, you point the phone at the sky. Step four, you tap something to learn about it. Step five, you can view the whole sky as a map. Step six, you save what you saw. Each of the next six slides shows one of these steps on a phone screen.
