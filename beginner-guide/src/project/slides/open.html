Step one. You open the app and it asks a single question: may it use your location? That matters because the sky looks different from different places on Earth, and it turns through the night. Your location plus the exact time is all SkyLens needs to know which stars and planets are above you. The location never leaves your phone, and there is no account to create. If you say no, it still works using a default place, and you can type your city in instead.
