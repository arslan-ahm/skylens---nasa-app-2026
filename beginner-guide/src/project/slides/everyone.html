SkyLens is built for people in the dark, outdoors, often in the cold. It has three looks. The normal dark look. A night-vision red mode: astronomers use red light because it does not spoil the eyes' adjustment to darkness, which takes around twenty minutes to build up. And a high-contrast mode with larger text for people with low vision. Buttons are deliberately big so you can use the app one-handed or with gloves on. The phone on the right switches between the three looks.
