Welcome. This guide walks through SkyLens from the point of view of someone who has never used a telescope or a star app. We will follow one evening with the app, screen by screen, and then look at how it works behind the scenes, in plain language. The diagram on the right is a little map of what SkyLens keeps track of around you: the Moon, the planets and even the Space Station, moving through your sky.
