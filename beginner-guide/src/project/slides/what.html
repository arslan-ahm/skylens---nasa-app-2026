Logbook

The whole idea in one line: point the phone up, and SkyLens tells you what you are looking at. It does three jobs. Find: labels float over the real sky. Understand: tap any label for plain-language facts and the best time to see it. Remember: keep a personal logbook, and optionally share observations with citizen-science projects. No account and no payment needed.
