SkyLens also brings in real NASA data: the Astronomy Picture of the Day, the list of asteroids passing close to Earth, alerts about solar flares and space storms, plus an open cloud forecast. The important part is the little label on every card. Live means it just arrived. Saved means you are offline and seeing the last copy the phone kept. Sample means demo data. Watch the phone: when the signal drops, nothing breaks, the labels simply switch to Saved. You always know how fresh the information is and where it came from.
