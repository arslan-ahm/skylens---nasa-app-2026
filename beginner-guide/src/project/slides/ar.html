HORIZON
Pointing
Step three is the magic moment. You hold the phone up like you are taking a photo, and labels appear over the sky behind the screen. Move the phone and the labels move with the real stars. The crosshair shows where you are aiming, and when something bright sits in it, it lights up and SkyLens tells you its name. The dashed line marks the true horizon, even when trees or buildings hide it. Phone compasses can be thrown off by metal or magnets, so if labels drift you centre the Moon or a bright planet and tap Calibrate.
