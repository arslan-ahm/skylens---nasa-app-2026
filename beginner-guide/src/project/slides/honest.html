02 — An honest promise

A sky guide, not a magic telescope

SkyLens is

  • A guide that names what you see
  • A planner for when and where to look
  • A logbook for your observations
  • A doorway to real citizen science

SkyLens is not

  • A replacement for a telescope
  • Live zoomed-in video of planets
  • A promise you will find a new planet
  • An AI that makes facts up
Before we start, an honest promise. A phone camera cannot zoom in on Saturn's rings, so SkyLens never pretends to. What it does do well: tell you what each dot of light is, when and where to look, and help you keep a record. When it explains things, it only uses checked data and shows where that data came from. And it never tells you that you discovered something new; instead it shows you how to check and report an observation properly.
