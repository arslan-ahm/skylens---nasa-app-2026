Step six closes the loop: saving what you saw. The form is short on purpose. You type what you saw, slide to rate how clear the sky was, and pick what you used: your eyes, binoculars or a telescope. Then save. You never type the date, time or location; SkyLens adds those by itself. Over weeks this becomes your personal sky diary, and it can be exported in a standard format that citizen-science projects accept.
