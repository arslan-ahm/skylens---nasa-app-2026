SkyLens says

"This may be worth reporting."

SkyLens never says

"You discovered a new planet."

For people who catch the bug, SkyLens offers a path from curiosity to contribution. You spot something, a bright meteor, a satellite, a star that seems to change. You record it, and the app saves the time, place and direction for you. SkyLens helps you check it against known satellites and sky events. Then, only if you choose, you can pass it on to citizen-science projects that collect exactly these kinds of observations. Notice the language: SkyLens says "this may be worth reporting", never "you discovered a new planet". Honest wording keeps beginners' trust, and keeps the science clean.
