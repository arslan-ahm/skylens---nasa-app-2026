Best to see now
Step two is the Tonight screen. It answers the beginner's first question: is there anything worth seeing right now? It shows the Moon's phase, because a bright Moon washes out faint stars. It shows when proper darkness begins. It shows a simple cloud forecast, where taller bars mean clearer skies. And it lists the brightest objects in your sky, easiest first, each with a direction and a height above the horizon. The real list depends on where you are and when you look.
