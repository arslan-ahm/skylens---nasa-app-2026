Sky map

Drag the time to plan your night

20:00
Step five is the sky map. Here you see the whole sky at once, as a circle. The outer edge is the horizon all the way round, and the centre is straight above your head. East is on the left, which looks odd at first, but it is exactly how the sky looks if you lie on your back with your head to the north. Drag the time slider and you can watch the sky turn around the pole star, so you can see what will rise later and plan when to go outside. This view needs no camera, so it works indoors and on a laptop too.
