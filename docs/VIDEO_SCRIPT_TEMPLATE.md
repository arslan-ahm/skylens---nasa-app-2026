## Recording tips

- [ ] Screen-record on a real device (Android: built-in recorder or `scrcpy --record`; iOS: Control Center).
- [ ] Use `DATA_MODE=mock` or pre-warm the cache so the network cannot break the take.
- [ ] Burn in captions. Many judges watch muted.
- [ ] Stay under the limit. Aim for 55 s on a 60 s cap.
- [ ] Use landscape 1080p or larger, with no notifications (Do Not Disturb on).
- [ ] Only use music you have the rights to (e.g. YouTube Audio Library).
- [ ] Show real app footage only. No mock-ups presented as working features.

## Example pitch: starting draft (adapt to chosen challenge)

> "Most people look up and see a few bright dots. They don't know which ones are planets, when the ISS passes, or whether tonight is even worth going outside.
> SkyLens turns any phone into a personal observatory. Point it at the sky and it labels stars, planets, and constellations, even offline. Tonight's Sky tells you what's visible and when, combined with the cloud forecast.
> NASA data such as APOD, near-Earth asteroids, and space weather comes with clear sources. When you've observed something, log it, and SkyLens points you to real citizen-science projects where your observations count.
> It's not a telescope, and it won't claim discoveries. It helps anyone start looking up, and keep looking.
> SkyLens: free, open, and built on NASA data."
